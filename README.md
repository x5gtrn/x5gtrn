## **🎵 Musical DNA**

Deeply influenced by **[Richie Hawtin](https://ra.co/dj/richiehawtin)**'s approach to sonic reduction and technological integration. His philosophy of minimal precision and system thinking shapes my transition from deck manipulation to original composition.

Equally shaped by **[Sven Väth](https://ra.co/dj/svenvath)**'s profound reverence for each individual track and his commitment to traditional DJ craftsmanship. Where technology drives toward seamless integration, Väth celebrates every record as a distinct sonic entity, weaving immersive worlds through careful selection and human intuition rather than digital manipulation. This respect for the integrity of individual compositions keeps me grounded in the timeless art of curation as I transition into original creation.
